Install Southend VoteWatch App

Add VoteWatch to your phone's Home Screen to launch it full-screen with its own icon—and receive new voting records automatically over the air.

🍏 On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
🤖 On Android (Chrome / Samsung):
  1. Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) in the browser corner.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
